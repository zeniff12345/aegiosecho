The Core Technical Pillars

How It Works

Three specialized agents ingest a raw distress signal, argue over the physical constraints of every proposed rescue, and hand a human operator a single decision to authorize — never the other way around.

Edge Resiliency

Every rescue decision is computed locally. No cloud dependency, no single point of failure — the system stays operational when the global grid is dead.

Agentic Consensus

Three specialized agents actively contest each other's constraints in real time and negotiate the safest available routing, instead of one linear guess.

Deterministic Safety

The agents handle the triage math and the physical-constraint arguments. Final operational authority never leaves human hands.

01

Ingest & Score

A distress signal comes in from a case profile — a transcript, location, and hazard tag. The Semantic Triage Node reads it for urgency keywords, life-threat level, and hazard indicators to compute a stress index, entirely offline.

02

Debate & Resolve

The Kinematic Logistics Router checks whether the proposed rescue asset can physically survive the trip — wind speed, blocked roads, landing-zone clearance — and objects when it can't. The Command Arbitration Agent weighs that objection against the Triage Node's urgency call and negotiates a resolution live, on screen, in the Swarm Debate Terminal.

03

Human Authorizes

The Commander's resolved plan is displayed on the tactical map — never executed automatically. A human operator reviews it and explicitly approves, denies with a reason, or holds it for later. Nothing moves without that click.
